# Materiały wizualne

- Logo i ilustracja Miedzianego Mostu pochodzą z projektu Archon. Wszystkie portrety bestiariusza przygotowano z plików `unity/Archon3D/Assets/Archon/Resources/EpicUI/Portraits`. Nazwy i opisy postaci opierają się na danych i kartach postaci z gry.
- Ujęcia plansz 2D i 3D oraz pojedynku pochodzą z galerii `previews/screenshots` projektu Archon. W obu widokach planszy pokazano tę samą turę.
- Ilustracje królestw w sekcji prób pochodzą z `unity/Archon3D/Assets/Archon/Resources/EpicUI/Scenes` projektu Archon.
- Kroje Cinzel i Crimson Pro użyte na stronie pochodzą z `unity/Archon3D/Assets/Archon/Fonts`, gdzie służą do nagłówków i opisów w grze. Ich licencje SIL Open Font License znajdują się w katalogu `fonts`.
- Ilustrację otwierającą wygenerowano wbudowanym narzędziem ImageGen na podstawie portretów Walkirii i Smoka oraz Miedzianego Mostu. Plik `hero-walkiria-smok-mobile.jpg` to kadr mobilny ilustracji `hero-walkiria-smok.jpg`.

## Prompt ilustracji otwierającej

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: widescreen key art for the official Polish landing page of Blades Between Turns. Produce the illustration alone, intended as the background behind separate HTML typography.
Input images in order: 1 original Walkiria portrait, character design reference. 2 original Smok portrait, character design reference. 3 original Miedziany Most illustration, architecture and amber/indigo lighting reference.
Scene: a single cinematic fantasy confrontation on the Copper Bridge, with the huge inlaid squares of its ancient stone board conspicuous in the lower foreground. One contested square emits a fine ring of golden light that reflects on neighboring wet stone. The sunlight-clad Walkiria is seen in a strong, readable three-quarter pose, her blond hair flowing, winged gold circlet, ivory-and-gold armor and the long spear from reference 1, confronting the large blue-violet dragon with long silvery horns and armored scales from reference 2. Both alert and in motion, one anticipatory instant before the duel; no contact, blood, injury or flame from the mouth.
Composition: cinematic 16:9 landscape concept art. Make the encounter the hero on the RIGHT: Walkiria centered around 59% of canvas width, dragon head around 81%, keep both faces between 52% and 85% width and around middle height so they hold up in a crop. Leave the left 38% spacious and quite dark with nearly black smoky architecture for a tall readable title. Keep the stone board perspective immediately apparent at bottom. Characters are the first readable silhouettes at thumbnail scale. Controlled atmospheric depth, delicate orange dust only around the old stone, editorial contrast, organic painterly illustration with detailed faces and armor, contemporary premium box art. Preserve the specific warm bronze against midnight cobalt palette. No logos, titling, lettering, icons, borders, or interface elements.
```
